About us

About Summit Tire & Wheels

Summit Tire & Wheels Ltd is a locally owned mobile tire service based in Penticton, BC. Instead of you getting the vehicle to a shop, the shop comes to you — the truck carries the tires, the jack, and the torque wrench to your driveway, your jobsite, or the shoulder of the highway.

We handle new and used tires, seasonal changeovers, wheel and rim work, and emergency roadside calls. The phone gets answered around the clock, because a blowout at two in the morning on Highway 97 doesn't wait for business hours.

You'll get a straight answer on what the vehicle needs and what it costs — including the times a good used set is the sensible call.
